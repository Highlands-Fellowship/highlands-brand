Logo Rules
  • → The circular H mark is primary for compact contexts — social profiles, signage, merch, app icons.
  • → Use the light (white) logo on dark backgrounds. Use the dark (navy) logo on light backgrounds.
  • → Approved backgrounds: white, Navy #0b1d41, Teal #55b6b2, and photo overlays.
  • → Do not stretch, recolor, add effects, or distort any logo.

Voice & Tone
How We Sound
Warm, welcoming, and clear — like a trusted friend from the congregation. Approachable without being shallow; grounded without being stiff.

We Sound Like

  • A welcoming neighbor who's glad you're here
  • Clear and direct — we say what we mean
  • Encouraging and hope-filled
  • Human, not institutional
  • Authentic — real stories, real people

We Don't Sound Like

  • A formal religious organization issuing pronouncements
  • A corporation hiding behind passive voice
  • Overly casual or flippant about serious topics
  • Trendy for the sake of being trendy

Agent Rules
Rules for AI Agents
When producing branded work, load brand-kit.json first. These are the governing directives from that file.
brand-kit.json is the single source of truth. AI agents must load and strictly follow these paths, colors, and rules.
All typography must use Roboto Slab (Google Fonts) — Roboto Slab Medium (weight 500) is the default weight.
Navy Blue (#0b1d41) and Teal (#55b6b2) are the primary brand colors. Yellow (#F5B731) is an accent — used sparingly, primarily in text.
Always use the white/light logo variant on dark or colored backgrounds. Always use the dark/navy logo variant on white or light backgrounds.
The Vision Gradient (teal → yellow) is reserved for campaign and sermon series graphics — not everyday communications.
Time formatting: 6pm not 6:00 PM. Date formatting: Thursday, Dec. 25 not Thursday, December 25th.
When in doubt about tone, ask: does this sound like a trusted, welcoming friend from the congregation?
